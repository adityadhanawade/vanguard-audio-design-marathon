# Vanguard Audio — Brand Identity & Launch Campaign

Solo submission for a 48-hour online Design Marathon (IIT Bhubaneswar). Team: **AVD Artifact** — Aditya Dhanawade.

## The brief

Design a complete brand identity and multi-channel launch campaign for **Vanguard Audio**, a fictional premium audio hardware brand launching modular acoustic headphones and home audio systems. The brief called for retro-futuristic minimalism, precision acoustic engineering, and luxury industrial aesthetics, held together as one design system.

Six deliverables were required, each on a different surface (packaging, digital UI, print, physical retail), all tied to that one visual system:

| # | Deliverable | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brand Style Guide | Logo system, color palette, type hierarchy, tagline/mission, usage & misuse rules |
| 2 | Packaging & Lifestyle Merch | Eco-friendly headphone box/sleeve, apparel mockups |
| 3 | Digital Presence & Web UI/UX | Landing page + an "Acoustic Craft" driver-engineering page |
| 4 | Product Collateral & Retail | Laser-engraved earcup pattern |
| 5 | Campaign Poster | A2 poster for the "Sound Sanctuary" launch event |
| 6 | Presentation | 5-slide deck covering concept, rationale, and deliverables |

## Creative direction — "Machined Minimalism"

Graphite, char, brass, stone, and bone tones, paired with Space Grotesk (structure) and JetBrains Mono (technical labels), built around a recurring dial/crosshair mark. It picks up the brief's own anchors, precision engineering and luxury industrial design, using the same visual language premium audio brands like Bang & Olufsen and Teenage Engineering rely on.
